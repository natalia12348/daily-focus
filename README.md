# Daily Focus

A motivating to-do app that helps you get things done, one small win at a time.

## Features

- **Today list.** Write down what you need to do today and tick it off as you go. Every task has a priority color: Urgent, Important, Medium or Someday. The most important tasks always sit at the top.
- **Daily progress.** A progress bar shows how much of your day is done, and a streak counter counts the days in a row you finished something.
- **Confetti.** Every task you complete is celebrated.
- **Pick one for me.** Can't decide where to start? One button chooses a single task for you.
- **Break it into steps.** Turn any big task into a few tiny steps with AI, then tick the steps off one by one.
- **Goals.** Type something you want to achieve, like "learn Agile", and AI splits it into stages and small tasks. You can also paste your own plan or write it by hand, delete what you don't need, and send any task to your Today list.
- **Habits.** Track anything you do every day, like Spanish, drinking water or reading. Slide the switch to mark today as done, and see which days of the week you showed up.
- **Boo the bear.** A dancing bear cheers you on and tells you YOU CAN DO IT.
- **Quote of the day.** A new motivational quote every day.
- **Your own space.** Sign in with your email and your tasks, goals and habits follow you to every device. Nobody else can see them.

See [SETUP.md](SETUP.md) to run or deploy your own copy.
